Rules

The Nexus is 1:12 later than the macro version. That is the whole price of this branch, and it is only worth paying when you have scouted a reason. Pool first, a wall on your ramp, or a gasless greedy Hatchery. Otherwise run the Three Nexus Chargelot.
Zealots tank, Adepts shoot. Zealots walk in first and eat the Queen shots. Adepts stay behind them and hit Drones: 22 damage to anything Light, two shots per Drone.
Shade out, do not fight out. The shade shows the landing spot before it commits, so a misread costs nothing. Leave at six speedlings; Adept speed is 3.5 against 6.58.
Drones denied is the win, not kills. Every Zergling and Queen he makes instead of a Drone is the build working. Leave when he commits and get the Stargate down on time.
No Resonating Glaives. The old version of this page bought Glaives at 4:10. That money is Charge now, and the Adepts are a three minute tool, not an army.
Warp Gate research still has to happen. It is what turns a 27s Zealot into a 13.5s one, and without it there is no army at 7:00. Cancel it if it is competing with the Stargate, then put it straight back. Research it, do not transform.
Do not let it become an all-in. PiG lost nothing here and still took a third at 4:40. If the poke is walled out, walk home and macro; you are on plan.
