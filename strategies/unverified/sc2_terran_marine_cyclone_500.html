Marine-Cyclone: a 1-base 5:00 hit with a CC behind it

The Terran half of the 2v2 five-minute punch: move out at 4:25 with 11-13 Marines and 2 Cyclones (both pop before you leave), and Stim completes at 5:00 sharp — the moment you arrive at the enemy gates alongside the Protoss Chargelot round. The natural Command Center starts the moment you move out, so a defended hit doesn't leave you behind.

Terran 2v2 1-base UNVERIFIED DRAFT patch 5.0.16

Yes, it's realistic

One Barracks with Tech Lab never stops making Marines from 1:46; a second naked Barracks joins ~3:06. Factory Tech Lab gives Cyclone #1 at 3:47 and #2 at 4:19 — both in the army before it leaves. 11-13 Marines + 2 Cyclones at the 4:25 move-out.

Gas is the binding constraint

The whole plan costs 450 gas, all spent by 3:47. Two Refineries (second at 1:50) mine ~525 by 4:00 (114/min each once staffed) — early enough that only the Factory is gas-gated; everything after is structure-gated. One gas, and this build does not exist.

CC behind the push

Cutting SCVs at ~23 (3:30) plus MULE income pays for the 400-mineral Command Center at the natural as you move out. It finishes ~5:36. If they hold the 5:00 hit, you're expanding, not dead.

Marine upgrades: pick one

The upgrade slot opens at 3:15 (Rax Tech Lab free, gas available); 3:20 is the 5:00-sharp choice. One Tech Lab runs one research at a time:

  • Stim (100/100, 100s) — start 3:20, done 5:00 sharp. Recommended: biggest swing in the fight, timed to arrival.
  • Combat Shield (100/100, 79s) — done ~4:39. Safest if you hate Stim HP-loss micro.
  • +1 Infantry Weapons (100/100, 114s) — done ~5:14, too late in this slot; only makes 5:00 (4:52) if it takes gas priority over Cyclone #1 and you build a 125-mineral Engineering Bay by ~2:33.

A second upgrade before 5:00 means cutting a Cyclone. Otherwise queue Combat Shield when Stim finishes (5:00 → done ~6:19, for the reinforcement wave).

Why only 2 Cyclones

Cyclones are 100 gas each and 32s each from one Factory Tech Lab. Gas mined by 4:00 is ~525; the build already spends 450 by 3:47. A third Cyclone (start 4:19, pop 4:51) would miss the move-out and delay Stim — reinforce with Marines instead, they're pure minerals.

Execution

  • Pull 2 SCVs with the push to repair Cyclones — they're your only tanky units.
  • Both Barracks keep producing during the attack; rally to the front.
  • 2v2 sync: shared resources unlock at 5:00 — your ally can help fund the CC/Orbital if the hit stalls.
The two ways this dies in the build phase

One gas, or the wrong add-on

One Refinery is not enough. On single gas you afford the Factory tech and either Stim or one Cyclone — not both. Refinery #2 by 1:50 is as load-bearing as the Barracks. And the first Barracks takes a Tech Lab, not a Reactor: without it there is nowhere to research Stim, and the Factory's own Tech Lab is busy being the Cyclone prerequisite.
