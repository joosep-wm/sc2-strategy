Rules

The cancel is deliberate, and it is the whole trick. Warp Gate research goes down at 2:30, comes back off at 2:34, and the refunded 50/50 pays for the Stargate at 3:00. Research restarts at 3:08 and lands 4:48. Start it once and keep it, and the Stargate is late; skip it entirely and there is no army at 7:00.
Research it, never transform. A researched Gateway builds a Zealot in 13.5s against a Warp Gate's 22s cooldown plus 4s warp-in. Rally, do not warp in. Transforming is a late-game positioning tool.
The Oracles are for the 7:00 fight, not for harass. Pulsar Beam goes on the Queens before the Zealots arrive: 175 HP and not Light, so Zealots grind on them. Between 4:30 and 6:30 the Oracles scout and take a stray Drone, nothing more.
One Stalker, one Adept, no Sentry, no Forge. Every other Gateway cycle in the game is a Zealot.
Pressure, not an all-in. If the third is walled, walk home, take the fourth and go Colossus. You are three bases ahead either way. Roach or Hydra means double Robo into Colossus; Ling or Bane means Templar Archives into Storm. One tech path, never two.
