Double Oracle vs Zerg: Wall-In Expand

Meta-standard PvZ on 5.0.16 (Harstem opening, 95% approval on Spawning Tool). Wall to the natural, expand behind it, two Oracles fly to the Zerg main for the queen + drones and the scouting that the muta loss proved we need. One practice rep so far (2026-08-08, vs AI): it proved the concept and failed the clock; see the rep review. Ladder PvZ is our weakest matchup (7-8 over the replay set), which is what this build is for.

Rules

The mission in the main: queen first if it is alone, then drones. Queens are not Light (DB: Biological, Psionic, 175 HP, 1 armor), so Oracles kill drones fast and queens slowly; two Oracles can finish a lone queen, and with it dead the drone line is free. If a second queen or a Spore is up, take drones on the edge or leave with the scout; defended queens are not the paycheck.
Spore seen = note tech, retreat, set the trap. Do not trade Oracles into a Spore. On the way out read gas count, Lair, Spire. Then both Oracles drop Stasis Wards in front of our natural wall, the trap that catches the counterattack the Spore usually announces. Standard 21s stasis on triggering units.
The Oracles are the scout. Every pass reads gas count and tech. 4+ Extractors by 4:30 = tech commit; Spire seen at T = Mutas from ~T+1:30: cannons in both mineral lines, Stalkers home. This is the muta insurance the wall does not provide.
Stasis Wards on the runby path once Oracles are idle; standard 21s stasis on triggering units. (Correction 2026-08-09: a third-party claim that 5.0.16 made stasis permanent is NOT in the official patch notes and was removed from this guide.)
The wall is load-bearing. In the replay set Zerg pools went down at 0:50-1:13 in seven of fifteen ladder games. The 0:34 wall Pylon and the 1:00 wall Gateway are not optional decoration; a hole here loses before the Stargate exists.
APM honesty: Oracle beam toggling under queen fire is the most micro this playbook asks anywhere. If both Oracles die with under 10 drone kills, revert to Chargelot Five and keep the wall.

First rep review (2026-08-08, Void Training vs AI)

The build died on the clock, not the concept. Observed: Nexus 1:52 (on time) but Stargate 5:44 (plan 2:49, 2:55 late) and Oracle #1 born 6:38 (plan: born ~4:12, DB build time 37s from a 3:35 start). Spores were up at 6:33-6:36 before the first Oracle ever flew, the exact scenario the retreat branch exists for, created by our own lateness. The Twilight at 3:49 came before the Stargate; vs Zerg the Stargate cuts the line. Drill order: Nexus 1:54 → Core 2:05 → Stargate 2:49 → everything else.

Follow-up: Chargelots + Void Rays, Mothership on top

Twilight ~5:00 → Charge; the Stargate never idles. After Oracle #2 it makes Void Rays; Gateways warp Chargelots behind the wall. Chargelots absorb, Void Rays kill the Roach/Queen layer the Zealots bounce off.
Mothership when the third pays for it. Practice-rep anchors: second Stargate 7:48, Fleet Beacon 7:34, Mothership started 9:11. Cloak field over the Chargelot-Void Ray army is the low-APM force multiplier; timings are replay-observed once and not yet DB-verified.
