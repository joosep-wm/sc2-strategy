Rules

Warp in, every 20 seconds. A Warp Gate makes a Zealot every 20 seconds and a Gateway every 27, so the old "research it, never transform" rule is reversed. Warp at a Pylon next to a Nexus: 3.6 seconds there, 11.4 anywhere else.
No cancel. The 2:30 Warp Gate cancel paid for a 3:00 Stargate on 8 workers. On 12 the research and the Stargate both fit by 2:31.
Two gases until the move-out. The main's two geysers pay for everything through Charge. The natural's gases cost Zealots before 6:06 and float gas after it, so they go down as the army walks.
The Oracles are for the fight, not for harass. Pulsar Beam goes on the Queens before the Zealots arrive: 175 HP and not Light, so Zealots grind on them. Until then the Oracles scout and take a stray Drone, nothing more.
Pressure, not an all-in. If the third is walled, walk home, take the fourth and go Colossus. You are three bases ahead either way. Roach or Hydra means double Robo into Colossus; Ling or Bane means Templar Archives into Storm. One tech path, never two.
