Rules

The Nexus is 1:22 later than the macro version. That is the whole price of this branch, and it is only worth paying when you have scouted a reason. Pool first, a wall on your ramp, or a gasless greedy Hatchery. Otherwise run the Three Nexus Chargelot.
Zealots tank, Adepts shoot. Zealots walk in first and eat the Queen shots. Adepts stay behind them and hit Drones: 22 damage to anything Light, two shots per Drone.
Shade out, do not fight out. The shade shows the landing spot before it commits, so a misread costs nothing. Leave at six speedlings; Adept speed is 3.5 against 6.58.
Drones denied is the win, not kills. Every Zergling and Queen he makes instead of a Drone is the build working. Leave when he commits and get the Stargate down on time.
No Resonating Glaives. That money is Charge, and the Adepts are a three minute tool, not an army.
Research once, then warp. Warp Gate research goes down with the Nexus at 2:47 and the Gateways turn into Warp Gates on their own when it lands. On 5.0.17 a Warp Gate makes a Zealot every 20 seconds against a Gateway's 27, so there is nothing left to gain by staying a Gateway.
Do not let it become an all-in. If the poke is walled out, walk home and macro; you are on plan.
