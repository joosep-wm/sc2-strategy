Marine-Cyclone: a 1-base 4:30 hit with a CC behind it

The same build re-timed for 5.0.17's 12 starting SCVs, which moves everything about 30 seconds earlier: move out about 4:00 with 11 Marines and 2 Cyclones (both pop before you leave), and Stim completes about 4:31, as you arrive. The natural Command Center now goes down at 3:47, before the move-out, so a defended hit doesn't leave you behind.

Terran 2v2 1-base UNVERIFIED DRAFT patch 5.0.17

Yes, it's realistic

One Barracks with Tech Lab never stops making Marines from 1:41; a second naked Barracks joins at 2:44. Factory Tech Lab gives Cyclone #1 at 3:21 and #2 at 3:53 — both in the army before it leaves. 11 Marines + 2 Cyclones at the 4:00 move-out, 14 by 4:30.

Gas is no longer tight

The whole plan costs 450 gas, all spent by 3:21. Two Refineries (second at 1:25) mine about 160 a minute each once staffed, so roughly 380 gas sits unspent at 4:00. Two Refineries are still required; one gas and this build does not exist.

CC behind the push

Cutting SCVs at about 23 (3:00) plus MULE income pays for the 400-mineral Command Center at the natural at 3:47. It finishes 4:58. If they hold the hit, you're expanding, not dead.

Marine upgrades: pick one

The upgrade slot opens at 2:51 (Rax Tech Lab free, gas available). One Tech Lab runs one research at a time:

  • Stim (100/100, 100s) — start 2:51, done 4:31. Recommended: biggest swing in the fight, timed to arrival.
  • Combat Shield (100/100, 79s) — done ~4:10. Safest if you hate Stim HP-loss micro.
  • +1 Infantry Weapons (100/100, 114s) — needs an Engineering Bay; even started at 2:51 it lands ~4:45, after arrival.

The spare gas now pays for a second upgrade: queue Combat Shield when Stim finishes (4:31 → done ~5:50, for the reinforcement wave).

Why only 2 Cyclones

Cyclones are 32s each from one Factory Tech Lab, so the Factory, not gas, is the limit now. A third Cyclone starts 3:53 and pops 4:25, which misses a 4:00 move-out. Take it only if you hold the move-out to 4:25; otherwise reinforce with Marines.

Execution

  • Pull 2 SCVs with the push to repair Cyclones — they're your only tanky units.
  • Both Barracks keep producing during the attack; rally to the front.
  • 2v2 sync: no Protoss page hits at 4:30 any more. The 3-Gate Robo punch (about 4:35) is the closest; the Three Nexus Chargelot walks at about 6:06.
The two ways this dies in the build phase

One gas, or the wrong add-on

One Refinery is not enough. On single gas you afford the Factory tech and either Stim or one Cyclone — not both. Refinery #2 by 1:30 is as load-bearing as the Barracks. And the first Barracks takes a Tech Lab, not a Reactor: without it there is nowhere to research Stim, and the Factory's own Tech Lab is busy being the Cyclone prerequisite.
