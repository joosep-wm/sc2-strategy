Chargelot Seven

Three Gateways, no Warp Gate research, all the gas into a Stargate and Charge. Twelve Chargelots and two to three Oracles walk in at 6:35 and the Queens die at 7:10.

DraftPvZ Patch 5.0.16rev 3 · 2026-09-20 Source: PiG VOD

The old Chargelot Five was a one base all-in off a 4:30 scout gate. This is not that. This is a three base macro build that happens to kill Zerg on the way past. You take a natural at 1:38 and a third at 4:28, you never stop making Probes, and the punch is paid for out of income rather than out of your economy.

Behind the punch

Six Gateways and the extra gases go down as the army leaves, not after the fight resolves. PiG queues them at 6:37 while the Zealots are still walking.
Then one tech path, never two. Roach or Hydra means double Robo into Colossus. Ling or Bane means Templar Archives into Storm. Fourth base instead if the map is open.

Rules

No Warp Gate research, and no transforming. A researched Gateway builds a Zealot in 14s on PiG's screen, but the 50/50 delays the Stargate past 3:00 and the whole build is gas starved until 4:00. Three plain Gateways with chrono is enough: PiG banked 771 minerals at 6:32 with production running.
Oracles are not a harass detour. They exist to delete Queens in the fight at 7:00. Send them in with the Zealots, not ahead of them. Between 4:30 and 6:30 they scout and pick off a stray Drone, nothing more.
Pulsar Beam before the Zealots arrive. Queens are 175 HP and not Light, so Zealots grind on them. Beam kills the Queens, Zealots kill everything else.
Pylons constantly. The failure mode is supply block at 5:30 with three Gateways queued. Two at a time from 3:55 onward.
This is a pressure, not an all-in. If the third is defended, pull back, take the fourth, and go Colossus. The build is three bases ahead either way.
2v2: same skeleton, but the third Nexus waits and the Oracles go to whichever ally is being hit. The 6:37 move-out is close enough to your 7:00 call with Joosep that the two land together. Pair it with his Liberator by 4:30.
